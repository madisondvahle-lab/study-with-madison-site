# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Nursing students preparing for or retaking the NCLEX use the public blog for practical study guidance.

## Product Purpose

The Study With Madison, RN website shares public NCLEX study guidance and information about tutoring. Blog readers should be able to understand the advice and identify a useful next step.

## Capabilities and Constraints

This repository is the public business website. Private student dashboards, individualized plans, assessments, question-bank administration, and student-specific data workflows belong in the separate NCLEX Success Center repository.

## Brand Commitments

The public business name is Study With Madison, RN. Use the user-provided logo at `public/study-with-madison-logo.png` across the public website. Preserve the existing navy, cream, peach, rose, plum, and sage brand palette, as well as the accuracy and educational intent of published article content.

## Evidence on Hand

Published blog posts and public tutoring information in this repository.
